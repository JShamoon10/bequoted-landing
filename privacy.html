Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

BeQuoted (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is built on a simple promise: your lore stays yours. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and the control you have over your information when you use the BeQuoted iOS app and this website.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and authentication details so you can sign in and sync across devices.
  • Content you create. The quotes, books, attributions, reactions, and comments you add are stored so we can show them back to you and the people you invite.
  • Group membership. When you join or create a shared book, we store which books you belong to and who you have invited.
  • Device & usage data. Basic diagnostic and performance data (like crash logs and app version) helps us keep the app stable.

How we use your information

  • To provide the core service: saving, organizing, and syncing your quotes and books.
  • To share content only with the people you explicitly invite to a book.
  • To send essential account and service notifications.
  • To diagnose issues, prevent abuse, and improve the app.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. There is no public feed and no advertising algorithm reading your inside jokes. Your private books are never shared with anyone you have not invited.

Sharing and visibility

Private books are visible only to you. Shared books are visible only to the members you invite. You control who joins a book and can remove access at any time. Removing a member stops their access to that book going forward.

Data retention

We keep your content for as long as your account is active. You can export or delete individual quotes, entire books, or your whole account at any time from within the app. When you delete content, it is removed from our active systems and purged from backups within a reasonable period.

Your rights & choices

  • Access and export your quotes and books at any time.
  • Correct or delete your content and account.
  • Manage who has access to each shared book.
  • Request a copy of the personal data associated with your account.

Children’s privacy

BeQuoted is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

Contact us

Questions about your privacy?

Email us at support@bequoted.app and a real human will get back to you.
