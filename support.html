Frequently asked

How do I create a shared book?

Tap the + button on your home screen, name your book, and invite people by link. Everyone you invite can add quotes and react.

How do I invite friends to a book?

Open the book, tap the members icon, and share the invite link. Anyone with the link you send can join that specific book — and no others.

Can I make a book private?

Yes. Private books are visible only to you unless you explicitly invite someone. You can switch a book’s visibility and remove members at any time.

How do I export or delete my quotes?

Head to a book’s settings to export it, or delete individual quotes, entire books, or your whole account from the app’s settings. Deletion is permanent.

I found a bug or lost some lore — help!

Email support@bequoted.app with as much detail as you can. If something isn’t syncing, include the book name and roughly when it happened so we can investigate.

How do I request a feature?

We love this. Send your idea to support@bequoted.app — a lot of BeQuoted was shaped by requests from people like you.
