Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a quote?＋

Open the app, tap “New Quote”, enter your text, customize the design, and hit “Save”.

Can I edit a published quote?＋

Yes. Find the quote in your “My Quotes” list, tap “Edit”, make changes, and republish.

How can I share my quotes?＋

Use the “Share” button to copy a link, post to social media, or send via email directly from the app.

What if I encounter an error?＋

Screenshot the error, note what you were doing, and email us at the address above. Our team will respond within one business day.

How is my data protected?＋

All data is stored securely with encryption at rest and in transit. See our Privacy Policy for details.

For additional help, visit our Help Center within the app or reach out via the email above.
