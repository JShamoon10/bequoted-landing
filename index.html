FAQ

Good questions.

Is BeQuoted free?+

Yes. You can capture quotes and create shared books for free. A future Pro tier will add larger books and extra customization, but the core lore-keeping will always be free.

Is it only on iPhone?+

BeQuoted is built natively for iPhone first. Android and web companions are on the roadmap — grab it on the App Store today and you’ll be first to know.

Who can see my quotes?+

Only the people you invite to a book. Private books are visible to you alone. There is no public feed and nothing is shared unless you choose to share it.

How many people can join a book?+

Shared books are built for real friend groups, so you can add everyone who was actually there. Larger community books are coming with Pro.

Can I export or delete my lore?+

Always. Your archive belongs to you — export any book or delete quotes and books entirely, anytime, from settings.

How do I get support?+

Email us anytime at support@bequoted.app or visit our support page. We read every message.
