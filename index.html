What is BeQuoted?＋

A private, shared home for the funny, heartfelt, and completely unhinged things your people say.

Can I make private quote books?＋

Yes. Every book is private by default, and you choose who can see or add to it.

Can multiple people add quotes?＋

Absolutely. Invite your people and build the lore together.

Can I save conversations?＋

Yes — dialogue quotes let you save the setup and the punchline.

Can I bring in old quotes?＋

Import from Notes, screenshots, or add them manually.

Is BeQuoted on Android?＋

BeQuoted is launching on iPhone first. Android is on the roadmap.
